# Scientific specifications and provenance

Only published written descriptions, permitted archive observations and station
metadata, and the two explicitly permitted guidance documents are used.
The paper PDFs and rendered reference pages are local reading/comparison copies;
they are excluded from GitHub. No author code, processed traces, numerical model
outputs, plotting scripts or separate figure assets are used. No plot is digitized.

* Hutko et al. (2006), doi:10.1038/nature04757, main text and supplementary
  legends (Nature MediaObjects MOESM2); supplementary PDF MOESM1 for comparisons.
* Lay et al. (2004), doi:10.1029/2004GL020300: response removal, receiver
  splitting correction, ScS source stacking (3–10 traces), zero-phase 0.3 Hz filter.
* Avants et al. (2006), doi:10.1029/2004JB003270: water-level source deconvolution.
* Avants et al. (2006), doi:10.1029/2005GL024989: ScS precursor interpretation.
* Thomas et al. (2004), doi:10.1029/2004JB003013, Table 2: 15 event dates
  (the number 7 occurs twice; the text says 14). All hypocentres used in computation
  are fetched separately from USGS, not copied from that table.
* Polet and Kanamori (2002), doi:10.1046/j.1365-246X.2002.01630.x:
  receiver splitting method and limitations. Station-specific corrections are
  not inferred by digitizing its maps.

The Nature supplement is decisive when it differs from earlier descriptions:
65–85 degrees, M6.0–7.2, 218–610 km, normalization to S, 0.2-degree/10-km grid,
4-second symmetric phase mutes, isotropic upward-going single-scattering legs.
The originally used station membership, source pulses, phase picks, and exact
profile endpoints are not tabulated. Configuration choices are documented as
assumptions, never fitted to the published image. Profiles approximate the named
directions and 1700-km CMB-scale span; endpoints are newly selected coordinates.
